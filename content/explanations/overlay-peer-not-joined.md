## What happened

The phone dialled a mesh address, but one of the two devices has not joined the mesh. A mesh address only exists between devices that have joined.

## The concept

A mesh VPN builds a small private network on top of the internet. Each device that joins gets an address on it, and only members can reach those addresses. It is a WhatsApp group: you can only message people who are in it.

## How to fix it

Join both the server and the phone to the same mesh.

## On a real line

Install the same mesh VPN on both devices and sign in to the same account on each. Check that both show as connected before you test.
