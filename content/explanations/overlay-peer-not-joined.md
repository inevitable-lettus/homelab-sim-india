## What happened

The phone dialled a mesh address. But one of the two devices has not joined the mesh. A mesh address works only between devices that have joined.

## The concept

A **mesh VPN** builds a small private network on top of the internet. Each device that joins gets an address on it. Only members can reach those addresses.

It is like a WhatsApp group: you can only message people who are in the group.

## How to fix it

Join both the server and the phone to the same mesh.

## On a real line

1. Install the same mesh VPN on both devices.
2. Sign in to the same account on each.
3. Check that both show as connected before you test.
